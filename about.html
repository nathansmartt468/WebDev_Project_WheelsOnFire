Meet John O'Reilly

Welcome to Wheels on Fire. My name is John O'Reilly and I've been passionate about cars for as long as I can remember.

I believe a well-tuned engine is a beautiful thing, and I take pride in finding cars that are as impressive as they are enjoyable to drive.

Our Philosophy

At Wheels on Fire, we want to help people find the perfect car while keeping the experience simple and enjoyable.

I'm a bit of a perfectionist. Maybe even more with cars than with people.
